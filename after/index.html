Nuestra historia / Our story

One griddle, one family, thirty-plus years.

Don Refugio opened El Sol in 1994 with a single plancha and his wife’s recipes from Jalisco. Today his kids run the trompo, and the masa is still pressed by hand every morning before the doors open.

Nothing here comes from a bag. The consomé simmers overnight, the horchata is cinnamon-steeped daily, and the salsa bar is still free — because that’s how Refugio did it.
